# Surefire Cyber

> Cyber incident response firm serving the cyber insurance ecosystem — carriers, brokers,
> breach-counsel law firms, and the organizations they cover. Delivers end-to-end,
> insurance-driven incident response: 24/7 intake and containment, digital forensics,
> in-house threat-actor negotiation, restoration, cyber intelligence, and claims-ready
> reporting. Launched 2022 with $10M led by Forgepoint Capital; headquartered in Maryland.

## API status

Surefire Cyber publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-29 there is no developer portal,
no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDK in any public registry, no
GitHub organization, and no `/.well-known/` document of any kind. The company's
"next-generation AI-enabled response platform" (announced 2026-02-09) is a delivery and
claims-coordination system for its own engagements and its insurance partners; it is not
offered as a developer product. The only machine-readable surface on the domain is the
WordPress core REST API at `https://www.surefirecyber.com/wp-json/`, which describes the
marketing CMS rather than any Surefire Cyber product, and is therefore not catalogued as a
company API.

## Services

- [Incident Response](https://www.surefirecyber.com/incident-response/): detection and containment, forensic investigation, threat-actor communication and negotiation, data mining, restoration.
- [Incident Response Retainers](https://www.surefirecyber.com/ir-retainers/): pre-negotiated rates and terms for priority access to responders.
- [IR Plans and Exercises](https://www.surefirecyber.com/ir-plans-and-exercises/): incident response plan development and tabletop exercises.
- [Cyber Intelligence](https://www.surefirecyber.com/cyber-intelligence/): threat-actor and campaign intelligence drawn from response engagements.
- [Resiliency Support Program](https://www.surefirecyber.com/resiliency-support-program/): pre-incident readiness services.
- [Report an Incident](https://www.surefirecyber.com/report-an-incident/): 24/7 intake.
